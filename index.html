Produce, vegetables and meat

Get your fresh food from Black farmers

There's no national Black-owned brand of fresh produce, meat or eggs on grocery shelves yet. So go straight to the farm. The Black Farmers Index is our guide for this part of the list.

Black Farmers Index

A free nonprofit directory of Black farmers across the country, from vegetable growers and cattle ranchers to beekeepers.

How to use this

Make it a habit, not a hashtag

Swap one aisle at a time

Start with coffee, snacks or soap. Next trip, add another aisle. It adds up fast.

Ask at the register

If your store doesn't carry a brand, ask the manager to stock it. Stores track requests.

Set up auto-delivery

Detergent, toilet paper and coffee all ship on subscription straight from the brands.

Pass it on

Send this page to one more person. That's how a family list turns into a movement.
